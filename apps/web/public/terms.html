What this is

Still Here is a quiet online space for reflection. It is a free service offered in good faith. It does not replace a visit to a temple or the guidance of a teacher.

How to take part

Please be considerate of the others in the hall. Do not use programs to enter the hall in bulk, to take places from others, or to disturb the service. We may limit or end access for anyone who does.

No guarantee

The service is provided as it is. It may be unavailable, change or end at any time, and we do not promise that it will be free of faults.

The pictures, sounds and models belong to their makers and are used under the licences listed under “About” in the temple.

Privacy

You can visit without an account. People in your hall can see your generated nickname, where your avatar stands, and which ritual you are doing. Your browser remembers your nickname and settings. You can clear them in Settings.

We keep technical logs to help run the hall and troubleshoot problems. Our hosting provider handles connection information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the site.
